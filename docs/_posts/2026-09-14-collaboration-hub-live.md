Repo and briefing hub are up at
[sarapis/undatacommons-nyc](https://github.com/sarapis/undatacommons-nyc), everything public.

The [briefing page](https://sarapis.github.io/undatacommons-nyc/) is written to be read by an AI
assistant as well as by a person — paste the URL into Claude and ask it to get up to speed.
Verified that flow actually works before calling it done, rather than assuming.

Two pages, two jobs. The briefing holds **current state** and gets overwritten, so it is always
true *now*. This feed holds **history** and never gets overwritten. When a finding invalidates
something in the briefing, post here *and* correct there.

Open decision that blocks the benchmark view design: do we compare NYC to peer **cities** (needs
a non-UN source — OECD metro, Eurostat Urban Audit, UN-Habitat) or to **nations** (free and
honest today: "NYC's rate sits between Portugal and Slovenia")? Logged in the
[decision log](https://sarapis.github.io/undatacommons-nyc/decisions).
